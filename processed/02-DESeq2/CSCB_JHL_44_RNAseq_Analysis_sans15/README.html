MultiQC

The MultiQC report was generated from the standardized and pre-packaged RNA-seq QC and analysis pipeline, nf-core/rnaseq. STAR and Salmon were used for alignment and quantification against Ensembl's GRCm38 reference index. Those raw gene-by-sample counts were then used for our custom downstream analyses. Extracting run4_mouse_star_salmon.zip and opening multiqc_report.html give more details about the quality of the sequenced samples and percent aligned reads.

DESeq2

The counts generated from nf-core were brought into R for the purposes of differential expression and GO enrichment and GSEA.

The R-log2-transformed counts were saved for the collaborator's own edification here:

  • rlog2_counts_allSamples_JH_RNAseq.csv

DESeq2 contrasted the differential expression as such:

  • Knockout condition 2 / knockout condition 1
  • Knockout condition 1 / wildtype condition 1
  • Knockout condition 2 / wildtype condition 2
  • Wildtype condition 2 / wildtype condition 1

CSVs were generated following the differential expression calculation by DESeq2:

  • genomeKO.conditioncondition2vs1.csv
  • genomeKOvsWT.conditioncondition1.csv
  • genomeKOvsWT.conditioncondition2.csv
